Privacy Policy

for the “Hoopline” app (iOS and Android) and this website · Last updated: 18 November 2026. This is a translation; the German version is authoritative.

In short: Hoopline has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your favourite teams and settings stay on your phone. Only if you turn on push notifications does our server store a push token for your device together with your teams, so it can send you tip-off and final-score alerts.

2. Data that stays on your device

The app stores on your device only: the teams you follow, your notification settings and the last selected league. This data leaves your device only if you use push notifications (section 4) and is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Game data and news via our server

Scores, schedules, standings, rosters, player stats and news are loaded from our own server (hoopline-api.94-dino.workers.dev), hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) as our processor. Each request necessarily transmits your IP address, the time and the requested address to Cloudflare. We do not analyse this data, build no profiles and have no access logging enabled. Our server fetches content from the EuroLeague and from the public RSS feeds of Eurohoops and Sportando without passing on any data about you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in delivering the app’s content reliably and securely).

4. Push notifications

Push notifications are optional. Your phone asks for permission first, and you can turn them off at any time under “Meine Teams” or in your system settings.

  • Your device receives a push token from Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA). It identifies Hoopline on your device only and contains no name, phone number or email address.
  • The app sends our server: the push token, the teams you follow, which alerts you want and your time zone.
  • Our server stores this at Cloudflare and sends alerts via Expo, which delivers them through Apple Push Notification Service or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Without a remote push connection (e.g. in a test build), the app only schedules local reminders on your device and sends nothing to our server.

Retention: deleted as soon as you unfollow all teams or turn off both push switches. If you delete the app, the push service reports the token as invalid at the next send and we delete it then. Records of which game alert was already sent (not linked to you) are kept for three days.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw it at any time by turning push off.

5. Images and links to third parties

Team logos and player photos are loaded directly from EuroLeague image servers (e.g. media-cdn.incrowdsports.com, media-cdn.cortextech.io), and news preview images directly from the news websites; these providers receive your IP address. When you tap an article or link, the provider’s website opens in a browser window and its own privacy policy applies.

6. What we don’t do

  • no account or login
  • no ads, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • no access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • no sale or sharing of data for advertising

7. Transfers to the USA

Cloudflare, Expo, Apple and Google are (also) based in the USA. Where the provider is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, transfers rely on the EU adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR); otherwise on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

8. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which processes your IP address to deliver the page and prevent abuse. We use no cookies or analytics here.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR) and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). Just email us. As we store no names or accounts, we can only link data to your device if you turn off push (which deletes it immediately) or tell us your push token. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

10. Children

Hoopline is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from them beyond what is described above.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The version published here applies.
